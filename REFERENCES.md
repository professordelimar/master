# References

**Author:** Nelson Ribeiro-Filho

This file lists principal references relevant to the historical gravity,
magnetics, potential-field processing, equivalent-layer, and numerical methods
represented in this repository.

## Potential-field geophysics

Blakely, R. J.  
*Potential Theory in Gravity and Magnetic Applications.*  
Cambridge University Press.

Hinze, W. J., von Frese, R. R. B., and Saad, A. H.  
*Gravity and Magnetic Exploration: Principles, Practices, and Applications.*  
Cambridge University Press.

Telford, W. M., Geldart, L. P., and Sheriff, R. E.  
*Applied Geophysics.*  
Cambridge University Press.

## Gravity and magnetic interpretation

Nabighian, M. N. (ed.).  
*Electromagnetic Methods in Applied Geophysics* and classical works on
potential-field interpretation and magnetic methods.

Grant, F. S., and West, G. F.  
*Interpretation Theory in Applied Geophysics.*

## Equivalent sources and inverse problems

Dampney, C. N. G.  
Classical work on equivalent-source representations of potential fields.

Menke, W.  
*Geophysical Data Analysis: Discrete Inverse Theory.*

Aster, R. C., Borchers, B., and Thurber, C. H.  
*Parameter Estimation and Inverse Problems.*

## Numerical and signal-processing methods

Press, W. H., Teukolsky, S. A., Vetterling, W. T., and Flannery, B. P.  
*Numerical Recipes.*

Bracewell, R. N.  
*The Fourier Transform and Its Applications.*

## Coordinate systems and geodesy

Snyder, J. P.  
*Map Projections—A Working Manual.*  
U.S. Geological Survey Professional Paper 1395.

## Software and legacy dependencies

The historical notebooks and modules make use of scientific Python tools such
as NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Pandas, Jupyter, and Basemap.

Some historical tests reference older geophysical software ecosystems. Those
dependencies may no longer be actively maintained or directly installable in
modern Python environments.

## File-specific references

Several source files and notebooks contain their own references, comments,
authorship notices, or notes identifying the formulation on which an
implementation was based.

Those file-specific notices should be preserved.

Additional references should be added as further historical material is
incorporated into the repository.
